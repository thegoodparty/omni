# Email outreach

Candidates (Win) and elected officials (Serve) email their opted-in contacts through Amazon SES. A send goes to a saved voter-file list with the email filter (`hasEmail`) forced on, so only people with an email address are counted, priced and sent to. Emails reach the people data only through a Serve organization's imported contacts (`src/contactUploads/`, read through the overlay in `src/peopleDb/`), so a Win list and a Serve list without imports count 0 and can't be confirmed. Design: TDD "2026-10-26 | Email outreach channel v1" (ClickUp doc `2ky4jq2q-20493`, page `2ky4jq2q-157273`); step lists, infrastructure and testing are on its Implementation Notes page (`2ky4jq2q-157293`). Payment goes through `src/paymentHolds/` (see its AGENTS.md).

## Invariants: do not break these

- **One sender at a time.** Only the holder of the `email_sweep_lease` row sends; the lease is the global SES rate limit. Don't swap it for a `CronLockService` minute claim, whose 30-second stale window is shorter than a sweep tick.
- **Recipients are claimed with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`** (raw SQL, since Prisma `updateMany` has no limit). Duplicate-send exposure rests on this claim.
- **A send starts only when its hold is `held`,** re-checked against Stripe through `PaymentHoldsService.getHold`. Every capture goes through `settle`; email implements the settlement adapter and never captures itself.
- **Email pages only when things are blowing up.** Expected bounces, complaints, 4xx and the SNS webhook's 503 never alert: `POST /v1/email/webhooks/sns` is in `ROUTES_WITHOUT_ROUTE_ALERTS` (`deploy/components/alerts.ts`), confirm's 402 for a declined card is in `EXCLUDED_STATUS_CODES` (`deploy/components/alerting/route-alerts.ts`), and the SES reputation rules keep their last answer on a query error and need 15 minutes above the line. A handled condition (an unset env var, a normal decline) logs `warn`, not `error`.
- **The warming cap (`recipientCap`) is frozen at confirm** and never raised.
- **The shared SQS queue is never reconfigured** (encryption, deduplication or new producers). Replies arrive by SNS at `POST /v1/email/webhooks/sns`, which enqueues them like any other producer. Other systems send to this queue too.
- **The SNS webhook is public and trusts nothing it hasn't verified:** signature, certificate host, then topic (`EMAIL_INBOUND_TOPIC_ARN`). It does no processing; it answers 200 only after the enqueue succeeds.
- **Replies are read only from this environment's bucket:** `process` rejects any `bucketName` other than `EMAIL_INBOUND_BUCKET` (`goodparty-email-inbound-<env>`, set by Pulumi).
- **Abuse limits.** `PATCH /sender` needs Pro on Win and sends at most 5 confirmation emails per organization per rolling 24 hours, then 429. Each send is an `OutreachEmailForwardConfirmation` row, counted under the sender row lock so it holds across tasks; a failed send deletes its row. Reply forwarding stops at 10 per replying address per sender per rolling 24 hours and 200 per sender per rolling hour, counted from `OutreachEmailReply.forwardedAt`. A capped reply is still saved (`forwardedAt` stays null) and logged at `info` with the sender and reason, never its content. A redelivered capped reply is re-checked and forwards if room has opened. Caps are soft under concurrent workers. Caps live in `emailOutreach.const.ts`.
- **Never forward a message from our own domain, or one carrying `X-GoodParty-Forwarded`.** That is the forwarding loop guard.
- **Per-email SES events never go through the queue.** Bounce, complaint and send counts come from SES's CloudWatch metrics, tagged per send. If delivery, open or click data is ever needed, add a configuration-set event destination to Firehose and S3; one message per email would back up every other job on the queue.
- **Each organization sends as its own SES tenant** (`OutreachEmailSender.sesTenantName`), so SES pauses one organization instead of the account. Every send passes `TenantName`.
- **Bounces and complaints are suppressed by SES's account suppression list,** not our table. `OutreachEmailSuppression` holds only unsubscribes.
- **Previews and tests never call real SES.** `VENDOR_CRONS_ENABLED` is true wherever vendors are mocked, so SES must be a mocked vendor. Dev sends only to allowlisted addresses and the SES mailbox simulator.
- **Suppressions are scoped by `organizationSlug`.** Office sends also honor the campaign's organization, through `ElectedOffice.campaignId`. Unsubscribe tokens carry the email and organization, never only a row id.
- **Theme keys are never edited in place.** Sends store only the key, so a changed theme gets a new key.
- **Copy is mode-keyed** (`docs/product-vocabulary.md`): voters and a campaign on Win, constituents and an office on Serve.

## Send lifecycle

`draft` → confirm → `pending_payment` (cap frozen) → `pending` once the hold is `held` or `scheduled`. A card declined at confirm (a send within 3 days, send-now included) is never scheduled: confirm answers 402 with the decline code, and the send goes back to `draft` with its own date and no cap reserved. Its hold row stays `hold_failed`, which the hold module's sweeps, attach-webhook retry and card route leave alone while the send is a draft. Confirming again with another card places a fresh attempt on that row (`updateCard`, `holdAttempt` already bumped) at the price that confirm froze. `pending_payment` otherwise means a placement still resolving; confirming again returns the stored outcome (a stored decline answers 402 and leaves it there, since only the confirm that froze a draft may revert it) and moves the send to `pending` (the due sweep also starts it if the hold is `held` by then). The due sweep starts a send whose hold is `held` (`in_progress`, enqueue prepare), and fails one whose hold is anything else at send time (releasing it first if it can), so it is never sent; prepare re-checks the hold too. Prepare writes the audience, then sets `preparedAt`; the sweep then sends. A send is finished when nothing is queued or sending, or when `stoppingAt` is set (a cancel, or the hold module's expiry guard). The settle sweep calls `settle`, then, once SES's numbers have had 5 minutes, deletes the template, advances warming if the send was clean, and sets `completed` (or `canceled` if recipients were left or nothing was sent).

- **The capture deadline ends a send as sent.** A hold lasts about 5 days, so the hold module's expiry guard settles a send still running about 30 hours after its send time (a long pause or a very large send). Both cancels (the owner's and staff's) set `stoppingAt` before they settle, so when `stopAndReport` is the one to set it on a send with recipients still queued, it is the deadline: once anything went out, the queued rest is marked `failed` in the same transaction and the send finishes `completed` with its real `sentCount`, charged exactly as any other settle. One that sent nothing keeps its queue, is charged nothing and ends `canceled`. A claimed batch that doesn't send (a retry, a stuck claim taken back) goes back to `queued` only while `stoppingAt` is unset, and to `failed` once it is set, under a share lock on the send row, so a batch in flight at the deadline can't leave a queue behind. The status page shows it as Sent to X of Y.
- **Pauses:** `bounce_rate`, `complaint_rate` and `manual` (a rendering failure or a batch SES rejected) from the sweep; `ses_tenant` when SES says the tenant is disabled (`MessageRejected` naming the tenant ARN); `ses_account` and `daily_quota` for the whole account, which the sweep clears itself once SES allows sending again.
- **A send that sent nothing is charged nothing** (the hold is voided). Otherwise the larger of 50 cents and a cent per email SES accepted.
- **The audience is written at prepare, in deliveries of about 2.5 minutes.** A delivery stops starting pages after `EMAIL_AUDIENCE_DELIVERY_BUDGET_MS`, saves its place (`audienceCursor`, the last person id written) and enqueues the same message again, deduplicated on the cursor, so no delivery outlives the queue's 5-minute visibility timeout. Each page is one transaction that locks the send and writes only while the delivery still holds the cursor and the send is neither resolved nor prepared, so a delivery overlapping past the timeout can't put back people the trim removed; it stops instead. Each delivery touches the send so the stalled-prepare retry doesn't start another chain. The list is read as it is at prepare, not as it was at confirm. A list deleted after confirm, or one that has emptied, writes nobody, and the settle sweep ends the send canceled with nothing charged.
- **Unsubscribes are honored mid-send.** Prepare drops them once, and every sweep tick deletes the queued recipients of each running send who have unsubscribed since, so a long or paused send never reaches them.
- **SES calls make one attempt** (`maxAttempts: 1`): the SDK's own retry of a timed-out `SendBulkEmail` could resend a batch SES already accepted. A failed batch goes back to `queued` for the next tick, which is the one remaining duplicate exposure.
- **Dev sends only to `goodparty.org` and the SES mailbox simulator** (`isBlockedByDevAllowlist`); other recipients are marked `failed`. The test send refuses other login addresses there.
- **SES names carry the environment** (`<env>-<organizationSlug>` tenants, `<env>-email-<id>` templates, `outreach-<env>` set, and the `outreachEmailId` tag value `<env>-<id>`). A live deploy with no `OTEL_SERVICE_ENVIRONMENT` refuses to send. Dev and prod share one account's CloudWatch metrics and their send ids overlap, so the tag is written and read only through `sendTagValue`.

## Gotchas

- Email drafts are `Outreach` rows with `status: draft`; the generic `POST /outreach` rejects `outreachType: email` (`createOutreachSchema`), so this module is the only way to make one.
- `POST /` is a full replace: omitted optional fields are cleared. It carries the list (`voterFileFilterId`, stored on the `Outreach` row) and `consentConfirmed`. Consent given for a list keeps its date while the same list is saved again, and a different list clears it. The save answers the draft without its `estimate`, so only `GET /:id` reads the people DB.
- **Consent is confirmed on every send** ("These people agreed to get email from my campaign/office"). The contact import's own consent box is generic, so it is not relied on for email.
- A Win draft without Pro gets no `estimate` and reads no voter data; confirm refuses it anyway.

- **Deployed dev sends through the SES mock,** and `VENDOR_MODE=live` there turns the crons off, so nothing deployed ever exercises real SES before prod. `scripts/email-dev-e2e.ts` runs the real prepare, sweep, auto-pause, finish and inbound services from a laptop against real dev SES and the mailbox simulator. The release-train spec `gp-webapp/e2e-tests/tests/app/dashboard/outreach/email-send.spec.ts` covers compose and the audience picker up to a Win list's empty state; no E2E account has people with email addresses, so the paid send is covered by the route tests here (`tests/outreachEmailAudience.test.ts` drives a 300,000-person list through mocked pages) and by `scripts/email-dev-e2e.ts`.
- `src/scheduled-messaging/` and `EmailService` (Mailgun) are transactional email. Don't build outreach on them.
- **SES won't delete a template a tenant still holds.** Finishing removes the tenant association first.
- Merge values go into SES's Handlebars template unescaped by us: Handlebars escapes the html part, and the sweep strips `<>{}` from names so the text part stays readable.
- Which address bounced isn't tracked per message. SES's `ListSuppressedDestinations` keeps every hard bounce and complaint with its date, so they can be matched back to recipient rows later.
