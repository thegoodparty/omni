# Win email flow

The email stepper. Win opens at `/dashboard/outreach/email` behind `win-email-outreach`; the Win hub's Email tile navigates there. Serve mounts `EmailFlow` with `SERVE_EMAIL_SURFACE` from the Serve hub's Email card, behind `serve-email-outreach`. Both flags are UI-only; gp-api's routes are not gated. Backend: `packages/gp-api/src/emailOutreach/AGENTS.md`.

## Gotchas

- Audience comes right after the goal, as in the other outreach flows. A draft needs its content, so the audience step saves nothing: the first save is the look step's Continue, carrying the list picked before it, and the schedule step saves again with the date.
- The schedule step looks like SMS's (a Send date calendar and a Send time select, with the browser's zone named through SMS's `timeZoneCaption`) but keeps email's rules: no notice period and no sending hours. The calendar offers today through 30 days out. Today's times (`emailTimeOptions`) are "As soon as it's paid" (the default), the hours still ahead and Custom time…; a later day offers every hour and Custom time…. Picking a day that doesn't offer the chosen time clears it, and Continue asks for one. "As soon as it's paid", or a time at or before now, saves no date, so the send goes out once it is paid (review says "As soon as it's paid"); a later time, up to 30 days out, saves it. gp-api sends one that comes due during payment right after confirm.
- **The form is split over two steps, one state.** No layout refuses anything written (`docs/adr/2026-11-06-email-photo-can-sit-at-the-bottom-of-every-layout.md`), so the write step holds Continue on all its fields' problems (`writeStepIssues`: subject and message required, field max lengths, an empty `## ` line, the photo) and the look step only on the button's (`lookStepIssue`): Continue is never disabled there: with an incomplete button a press saves nothing and shows what holds it under the button (the text or link it still needs, or the link's error), the way the write step and SMS do. Before a press, text with no link gets a prompt for the link and a typed link is checked as it is typed. `hasButton` shows the button when it was added (`EmailComposeState.buttonAdded`) or already has text or a link, so a saved or AI-drafted button is never hidden. Both steps edit the flow's one `EmailComposeState`, so Back and Continue keep everything.
- **Compose is a form; the email is blocks.** The form never shows blocks: `toContent` builds them in the order the layout usually has them (photo, Title as the H1, the message, button; `long_update` puts the photo under the H1), and a photo set At the bottom goes last in every layout. Title and Button are optional in every layout and left out while empty. In the message a blank line splits paragraphs and a line starting with `##` is an H2 (spaces before or after the mark don't matter). There are no dividers in the form, so `fromContent` drops them. A layout switch keeps every value. Errors are keyed by field, from `EmailContentDraftSchema` while typing and `EmailContentSchema` after a Continue press (for the button, once a link is typed); the look step's Continue saves only once the latter passes.
- **AI draft.** One auto request on first open, applied only while nothing is written (an uploaded photo or a typed link don't count) and only while the write step is still open; its blocks fill the form through `fromContent`, keeping the user's layout, an uploaded photo and a typed button link. While the first draft loads the message is read-only with a Drafting placeholder. A tone pick rewrites right away, as in the other channels: an untouched draft is redrafted in the new tone, the author's own words are improved in it, with Undo. Regenerate rewrites right away, edited or not, with Undo, as in the other channels. Improve sends the form as the draft fields and puts the reply back into Subject, Title, Message and the button text, with Undo in the message's bar; it is off over the endpoint's 10,000-character body. Dictation and the merge-tag buttons insert into the message.
- A confirm or test send that refuses the content names a block ("Block 4: …"). The review step's Fix the email, or a 400 at confirm, takes the flow back to the step holding the field that block came from (the look step for the button, the write step for the rest), with the message on that field (in the form's words when the local check finds the same problem) until the next edit.
- **Leaving.** Before the first save, Exit asks before discarding. Once the look step has saved the draft and nothing has changed since the last save, Exit closes without asking and shows a "Draft saved" toast, since the draft waits in the history. An edit after the last save (going back to the write step, a new send time) makes Exit ask again, so it is never thrown away under a "saved" message.
- `POST /v1/outreach/email` is a full replace. The flow re-sends every field on each save.
- **A draft reopens at `/dashboard/outreach/email?draft=<id>`.** The history drawer's Schedule email (`RESUME_COPY.email`) and the status page's footer CTA on a draft both go there. `EmailFlowPage` reads `GET /:id` once; anything but a `draft`, or an id that fails to load, is replaced with `/dashboard/outreach/email/<id>`. `loadEmailDraft` fills every step back in, since the next save replaces the whole draft and erases whatever was not loaded: the goal (a preset by its label, otherwise custom), `fromContent` for the form, the list (`preselectedListId`), the send time (an hour the picker lists, otherwise Custom time) and the id. It opens on the first step not done: goal, audience (no list), write, look, then schedule when there is no date or it has passed (a passed time loads as "As soon as it's paid"; a missing date can't say whether one was picked), otherwise review. Until the saved lists load a save keeps the draft's list; a list that is gone once they load sends the flow back to the audience step. `EmailFlow.test.tsx` asserts a reopened draft saves back exactly what it loaded.
- The audience step counts a list's people with an email address from the list detail's `email` reachability count. Only imported contacts have emails, so its caption says so and links to `IMPORT_CONTACTS_HREF` (the contacts page with the import wizard open). Email's `reachLine` makes the picked list and the All voters/All constituents row one plain count ("10 people with an email address"), never voters and new contacts apart. Saved lists in the popover show no count: there is no batched count endpoint. A list with no one to email says so and Continue stays off. There is no consent box: consent to email is the contact import's (`docs/adr/2026-11-02-email-consent-affirmed-at-contact-import.md`).
- The warming cap on the audience step is the sender's `availableCap`: the warming step's cap less what the sender's other unfinished sends reserved, the cap confirm will freeze, so its "Up to $X" never tops Review's. The review step reads gp-api's `estimate` (`audienceCount`, `recipientCap`, `priceCents`), counted server-side; gp-api freezes the real cap at confirm.
- The AI draft, image upload and test send call C2's routes (`/draft`, `/images/presign`, `/:id/test`). The image goes straight to S3 with the presigned form fields, and an image block's `url` must be the returned `publicUrl`; gp-api rejects any other URL. The test send needs the saved draft id, so it lives on the review step.
- A card declined at confirm answers 402 (`EmailConfirmDeclined`) and the send stays a draft. `EmailPayStep` turns that into the card component's decline state, and every retry goes through confirm again (the component keeps using `savePaymentMethod`), never the hold's card route, which refuses a draft. Back stays offered after a decline, since nothing left draft.
- A `pending_payment` send (a placement still resolving) takes a new card on `EmailStatus` through the hold's route, then confirm with `card-on-file` moves it to `pending`. A `pending` send with `cardDeclined` takes it through the same route and then only refetches: it is already confirmed, and the hold module places the new card on schedule.
- The public unsubscribe and forward-confirm pages live in `app/email/` and are in the middleware's public-route list. Their GET loads state only; the button POSTs.
