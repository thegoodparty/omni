# Win email flow

The email stepper. Win opens at `/dashboard/outreach/email` behind `win-email-outreach`; the Win hub's Email tile navigates there. Serve mounts `EmailFlow` with `SERVE_EMAIL_SURFACE` from the Serve hub's Email card, behind `serve-email-outreach`. Both flags are UI-only; gp-api's routes are not gated. Backend: `packages/gp-api/src/emailOutreach/AGENTS.md`.

## Gotchas

- Compose comes before audience because a draft needs a subject and body, and the audience step saves the draft with its list.
- **Compose is a block editor, not a page builder.** A layout pick on untouched content loads that preset's starter blocks; on written content it converts them and keeps every word (a heading becomes a paragraph in `personal_letter`, more than two paragraphs join into two in `short_cta`), and only extra photos, extra buttons and dividers can be dropped. A notice says what changed, with Undo. Keeping every word means an email can be too long for the new layout (text over `short_cta`'s 600 characters, or over 30 blocks); that switch is refused with a message instead. Add a block offers a kind only where inserting it breaks no rule that holds now, and move/remove are disabled the same way, so the editor can't reach a state the draft schema refuses. A `personal_letter` photo moves from one end straight to the other, since every spot in between breaks its rule. Inline errors use `EmailContentDraftSchema` while typing and `EmailContentSchema` after a Next press; Next saves only once the latter passes.
- **AI draft.** One auto request on first open, applied only while nothing is written (an uploaded photo or a typed link don't count) and only while compose is still open, fitted to the layout the user has by then, keeping an uploaded photo and a typed button link. Regenerate asks first when the content differs from what the starter or the last draft left (`EmailComposeState.pristine`, kept in the flow's state so it survives Back and Next). Improve sends the email as the draft fields and puts the reply back in place: each paragraph takes back as many reply paragraphs as it sent (one block can hold blank lines), the last takes any extra, and paragraphs the reply merged away are removed, with Undo. A reply that breaks a limit (a headline over 120 characters, `short_cta`'s 600) is dropped with a message, and Improve is off over the endpoint's 10,000-character body. Dictation inserts into the paragraph whose mic was pressed.
- A confirm or test send that refuses the content names a block ("Block 4: …"). The review step's Fix the email, or a 400 at confirm, takes the flow back to compose with that message on the card until the next edit.
- `POST /v1/outreach/email` is a full replace. The flow re-sends every field on each save.
- The audience step counts a list's people with an email address from the list detail's `email` reachability count. Only a Serve organization's imported contacts have emails, so a Win list reads 0: the step says no one in the list has an email address yet and Next stays off. Consent is tied to the list it was given for, so picking another list asks again.
- The warming cap on the audience step is the TDD ladder indexed by `sender.warmingStep`. The review step reads gp-api's `estimate` (`audienceCount`, `recipientCap`, `priceCents`), counted server-side; gp-api freezes the real cap at confirm.
- The AI draft, image upload and test send call C2's routes (`/draft`, `/images/presign`, `/:id/test`). The image goes straight to S3 with the presigned form fields, and an image block's `url` must be the returned `publicUrl`; gp-api rejects any other URL. The test send needs the saved draft id, so it lives on the review step.
- A card declined at confirm answers 402 (`EmailConfirmDeclined`) and the send stays a draft. `EmailPayStep` turns that into the card component's decline state, and every retry goes through confirm again (the component keeps using `savePaymentMethod`), never the hold's card route, which refuses a draft. Back stays offered after a decline, since nothing left draft.
- A `pending_payment` send (a placement still resolving) takes a new card on `EmailStatus` through the hold's route, then confirm with `card-on-file` moves it to `pending`.
- The public unsubscribe and forward-confirm pages live in `app/email/` and are in the middleware's public-route list. Their GET loads state only; the button POSTs.
