# Win email flow

The email stepper. Win opens at `/dashboard/outreach/email` behind `win-email-outreach`; the Win hub's Email tile navigates there. Serve mounts `EmailFlow` with `SERVE_EMAIL_SURFACE` from the Serve hub's Email card, behind `serve-email-outreach`. Both flags are UI-only; gp-api's routes are not gated. Backend: `packages/gp-api/src/emailOutreach/AGENTS.md`.

## Gotchas

- Audience comes right after the goal, as in the other outreach flows. A draft needs its content, so the audience step saves nothing: the first save is the look step's Next, carrying the list picked before it, and the schedule step saves again with the date.
- The schedule step's Day and Time open on today and now. A time at or before now when Next is pressed saves no date, so the send goes out once it is paid (review says "As soon as it is paid"); a later time, up to 30 days out, saves it. gp-api sends one that comes due during payment right after confirm.
- **The form is split over two steps, one state.** No layout refuses anything written (`docs/adr/2026-11-02-email-layouts-shape-content-but-never-refuse-it.md`), so the write step holds Next on all its fields' problems (`writeStepIssues`: subject and message required, field max lengths, an empty `## ` line, the photo) and the look step only on the button's, which show after a Next press. Both steps edit the flow's one `EmailComposeState`, so Back and Next keep everything.
- **Compose is a form; the email is blocks.** The form never shows blocks: `toContent` builds them in the order the layout usually has them (photo, Title as the H1, the message, button; a `personal_letter` photo first or last as picked). Title and Button are optional in every layout and left out while empty. In the message a blank line splits paragraphs and a line starting with `## ` is an H2. There are no dividers in the form, so `fromContent` drops them. A layout switch keeps every value. Errors are keyed by field, from `EmailContentDraftSchema` while typing and `EmailContentSchema` after a Next press; the look step's Next saves only once the latter passes.
- **AI draft.** One auto request on first open, applied only while nothing is written (an uploaded photo or a typed link don't count) and only while the write step is still open; its blocks fill the form through `fromContent`, keeping the user's layout, an uploaded photo and a typed button link. Regenerate asks first when the writing differs from what the last draft left (`EmailComposeState.pristine`, kept in the flow's state so it survives Back and Next). Improve sends the form as the draft fields and puts the reply back into Subject, Title, Message and the button text, with Undo; it is off over the endpoint's 10,000-character body. Dictation and the merge-tag buttons insert into the message.
- A confirm or test send that refuses the content names a block ("Block 4: …"). The review step's Fix the email, or a 400 at confirm, takes the flow back to the step holding the field that block came from (the look step for the button, the write step for the rest), with the message on that field (in the form's words when the local check finds the same problem) until the next edit.
- `POST /v1/outreach/email` is a full replace. The flow re-sends every field on each save.
- The audience step counts a list's people with an email address from the list detail's `email` reachability count. Only imported contacts have emails, so its caption says so and links to `IMPORT_CONTACTS_HREF` (the contacts page with the import wizard open). Email's `reachLine` makes the picked list and the All voters/All constituents row one plain count ("10 people with an email address"), never voters and new contacts apart. Saved lists in the popover show no count: there is no batched count endpoint. A list with no one to email says so and Next stays off. There is no consent box: consent to email is the contact import's (`docs/adr/2026-11-02-email-consent-affirmed-at-contact-import.md`).
- The warming cap on the audience step is the TDD ladder indexed by `sender.warmingStep`. The review step reads gp-api's `estimate` (`audienceCount`, `recipientCap`, `priceCents`), counted server-side; gp-api freezes the real cap at confirm.
- The AI draft, image upload and test send call C2's routes (`/draft`, `/images/presign`, `/:id/test`). The image goes straight to S3 with the presigned form fields, and an image block's `url` must be the returned `publicUrl`; gp-api rejects any other URL. The test send needs the saved draft id, so it lives on the review step.
- A card declined at confirm answers 402 (`EmailConfirmDeclined`) and the send stays a draft. `EmailPayStep` turns that into the card component's decline state, and every retry goes through confirm again (the component keeps using `savePaymentMethod`), never the hold's card route, which refuses a draft. Back stays offered after a decline, since nothing left draft.
- A `pending_payment` send (a placement still resolving) takes a new card on `EmailStatus` through the hold's route, then confirm with `card-on-file` moves it to `pending`.
- The public unsubscribe and forward-confirm pages live in `app/email/` and are in the middleware's public-route list. Their GET loads state only; the button POSTs.
