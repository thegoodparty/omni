Still being built

This is a first version, shared to find out what you can do with it. What is not finished:

  • Eight more flows are located but not yet drawn. Each needs its step list read out of the code once; the list is below.
  • Page zones are inferred. A flow's steps are declared in code; a page's zones are not, so on Campaign Manager and Campaign Tracker the grouping is a reading of each event's own description, not a fact.
  • Per-step volume on the outreach wizards needs a property breakdown in Amplitude that the snapshot does not hold, so those steps show the event's total, not the step's.
  • Refresh is manual. The explorer refreshes itself twice a week; this page does not yet, so its snapshot date above is the one to trust.

Located, not yet drawn

    Coverage here is a best guess. An event missing from a step can mean uninstrumented, instrumented but unanchored, or a step that does not warrant one. This view separates the first two and cannot yet judge the third. Something wrong or missing? Use the button at the top; it comes straight to me. Steps come from each flow's own config in gp-webapp; event status, volume, weekly series and provenance from the explorer snapshot; routes from event_anchors.json.
