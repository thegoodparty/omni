# Sending an Elected Official Sign-In Link (Sales Guide)

This is a quick way to invite an elected official to set up their account. From their HubSpot contact record, you click one button and we **email them a secure, one-click sign-in link**. They click it and get walked through a short setup for their office. That's it — no passwords, nothing to copy or paste.

## How to send a link

1. Open the **contact record** in HubSpot for the elected official.
2. Make sure the contact has a **first name, last name, and email** filled in. (If any are missing, the button won't work until you add them.)
3. Find the **"Serve Magic Link"** card on the record.
4. Click **"Generate & send magic link."**
5. You'll see a green **"Email sent"** confirmation. Done — the link is on its way to their inbox.

That's the whole job. The official gets the email, clicks the link, and lands in a short guided setup for their elected office.

## Good to know — Do's and Don'ts

- ⚠️ **DON'T click the link yourself to "test" it.** The link works **only once**. If you click it, you use it up — and when the real person clicks theirs, they'll get an "invalid / already used" error. Leave it for them.
- ✅ **Need a fresh link?** Just click **"Generate & send"** again. It sends a brand-new link, no problem.
- ⏳ **The link expires after about a week.** If too much time passes, generate a new one.
- 📋 **If you see a yellow "auto-send failed" warning,** the email didn't go out. In that case, use the **"Copy link"** button on the card and send the link to the official yourself.
- 🏛️ **If we already have their office on file, we pre-fill it for them** — so their setup is even faster. If not, no worries, they can still finish setup.
- ❓ **Button is greyed out?** It's almost always a missing first name, last name, or email on the contact. Add those and reload the card.
